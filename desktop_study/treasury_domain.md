# Treasury domain and proxy boundaries

Reviewed 2026-09-27. The [interagency liquidity-risk policy statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/interagency-policy-statement-on-funding-and-liquidity-risk-management.htm) emphasizes prospective cash-flow assessment, funding diversification, a cushion of liquid assets, stress testing, and contingency funding plans. The [2023 addendum linked from SR 10-6](https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2010/sr1006.htm) reinforces actionable contingency plans.

TreasuryShield predicts quarter-ahead public-data proxies for deposit change, borrowing substitution, and liquid-buffer change. A quarterly Call Report cannot reveal intraday payments, contractual maturity ladders, collateral eligibility, deposit account relationships, bilateral funding capacity, or management actions. Accordingly, 7-, 30-, and 90-day outputs are **research stress scenarios** using explicit assumptions and aggregate weekly [H.8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h8/about.htm) shapes. Peak funding need and runway are **proxies**, not LCR, NSFR, RLAP, RLEN, or actual bank liquidity.

Potential stress transmission is deposit runoff -> replacement borrowing or asset sales -> liquid-resource use. The scenario engine will examine institution-specific, market-wide, and combined shocks, with dependence and capital-liquidity sensitivities. Recovery actions will have capacities, haircuts, lags, and costs. None of the predictive associations will be described as causal.

The [New York Fed harmonized data](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/banking_research/balance-sheets-income-statements) provide a long bank-level history, but public quarterly observations cannot resolve a fast run at weekly or daily frequency. Its [construction documentation](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/banking_research/balance-sheets-income-statements/README) notes form transitions and selected imputations in older periods. This limits literal real-time interpretation. The [FDIC BankFind API](https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs) supplies external structure and failure information; disappearance from the panel alone is never a failure label.
