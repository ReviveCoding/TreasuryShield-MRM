# Regulatory and guidance matrix

Reviewed 2026-09-27. This is a public-data research project, not a representation of regulatory compliance or an internal bank liquidity measure. Applicability depends on the institution and intended use.

| Current source | Relevant principle | TreasuryShield implementation and test | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Federal Reserve SR 26-2, revised model risk management guidance (2026)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm) and [interagency text](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm) | Risk-based governance, effective challenge, conceptual review, outcome analysis, monitoring, inventory and documentation | Separate development and validation modules; challenger models; locked out-of-time evaluation; findings register; monitoring simulation; model inventory | SR 26-2 supersedes SR 11-7. The letter says it is expected to be most relevant to organizations over $30 billion in assets; it is guidance, not a prescriptive pass/fail standard. |
| [SR 10-6, funding and liquidity risk management](https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2010/sr1006.htm) and [interagency policy statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/interagency-policy-statement-on-funding-and-liquidity-risk-management.htm) | Cash-flow projections, diversified funding, liquid-asset cushions, stress and actionable contingency funding plans | Deposit/funding/liquid-asset distributions; transparent proxy stress engine; constrained recovery actions and activation lags | Public filings lack internal cash-flow, collateral, and contingency-funding details. |
| [SR 12-7, stress testing guidance](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1207.htm) | Multiple forward-looking stress methods, reverse stress, clear actionable results, capital-liquidity interaction | Institution, market, combined and reverse stress; dependence sensitivity; capital-liquidity proxy | The guidance described in SR 12-7 applies to covered organizations over $10 billion; this study does not implement their regulatory stress test. |
| [FFIEC first-quarter 2026 Call Report instructions and filing dates](https://www.ffiec.gov/sites/default/files/data/reporting-forms/FFIEC031_FFIEC041_FFIEC51_FIL10_202604.pdf) | Report dates precede submission and public availability | Separate quarter end, release proxy and forecast origin; enforce feature-information-date checks | Exact historical amendment vintages cannot be inferred from the current restated bulk files. |
| [Federal Reserve H.8 description](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h8/about.htm) | Weekly H.8 is an estimated aggregate balance sheet | Calibrate system context and weekly scenario shapes only | H.8 is not a bank-specific weekly panel. |

The five stress-testing principles in SR 12-7 and the 2026 model guidance inform the design; they are not asserted as requirements for every bank in the research universe.
