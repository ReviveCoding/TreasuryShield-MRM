Information clock and universe

Commercial bank RSSD ID and quarter are the main unit. Assets, deposits, call-report lags, source vintage, report form, and M&A flags are carried explicitly. Bank-origin features must be available by the forecast origin; labels require their future quarter and availability date. The Core historical uninsured quantity is a proxy, distinct from FFIEC direct values.

Forecast target and score

For horizon h, y(i,t,h)=log(Deposits(i,t+h)/Deposits(i,t)). Q05 pinball is max(0.05(y−q),−0.95(y−q)); lower-tail coverage is the proportion y≤q. Results include equal-bank, asset-weighted, and deposit-weighted metrics, MAE/RMSE, tail severity, weighted interval score, and quantile-crossing checks. M&A events are excluded on the primary path.

Model fitting and inference

Numerical medians, clipping/scaling, peers, HMM parameters, and model weights are fit within training folds. Forward HMM probabilities depend only on current and earlier macro information. Adaptive calibration, where used, updates only from targets published strictly before the current origin; its algorithm was fixed before final scoring. The invalid negative controls intentionally violate these rules and are isolated.

Bootstrap and uncertainty

Primary paired inference resamples circular four-quarter time blocks with all banks in each selected block. Bank-cluster and crossed bank/time-block resampling are sensitivity checks. Few holdout time blocks limit precision; a confidence interval is not evidence of stationarity.

Stress and optimization equations

Peak funding need is max over 7/30/90-day (net-outflow proxy minus liquid-resource proxy), floored at zero for shortfall. Four dependence methods vary the joint deposit, borrowing, and liquid-buffer draws. Reverse stress solves the minimum deposit runoff for a 30-day shortfall under a haircut/access grid. Recovery minimizes assumed cost under liquidity gap, capacity, timing, and haircut constraints.

Secondary four-quarter budgeting extension

Persistence, four-quarter trend, ridge, bank-only boosted, and regime-aware boosted estimates are tested on 2022–23 origins. The NII field is explicitly partial: loan/security interest income less selected funding interest expense. Other-borrowing interest expense is unavailable in the modern harmonized source and is omitted, so this proxy may overstate complete NII. An equal-dollar accounting projection enforces assets = liabilities + equity and reports the accuracy change from reconciliation.
